KazumiLite-muOS 0.2.3-r2

This is an unofficial muOS client. It is not an official Predidit/Kazumi
build and does not include Kazumi's copyrighted application icon.

The compatible rule data contract is based on KazumiRules, distributed under
the MIT License. A copy is included as KazumiRules-MIT.txt:
https://github.com/Predidit/KazumiRules

The bundled font is Source Han Sans CN Regular from Adobe, redistributed under
the Apache License, Version 2.0. A copy of the license is included as
Apache-2.0.txt.

The application does not provide, upload, or store video content.
